FAQ

Questions

What is Pebble Crows?

A collection of 2,000 fully on-chain pixel crows on Elysium, the Hyperliquid L2 by Kinetiq. The supply is minted in 10 pitchers of 200 crows.

What does the whitelist give me?

Every wallet that completes the steps is selected for the whitelist. Each crow you mint earns 200,000 $PEBBLE, sent after the mint sells out.

Does joining the whitelist cost anything?

No. You complete a few steps on X and submit your wallet address. We never ask for a signature, a transaction or your seed phrase. Anyone who asks for those is not us.

Who gets the Elysium airdrop?

25% of $PEBBLE is shared by wallets with at least 3 transactions on Elysium mainnet. The more transactions a wallet made, the bigger its share. Invite 10 friends to double it, or 30 friends to triple it. Share your card on X for an extra 5%.

When can I claim, and is it locked?

Claims open after the mint sells out. Every airdrop is 100% unlocked. No vesting, no waiting weeks.

What do Kalos holders get?

5% of $PEBBLE is shared across the 1,500 Kalos NFTs, based on a snapshot. The more Kalos you hold, the more $PEBBLE you get.

How much does a crow cost?

Pitcher 1 costs 0.0015 HYPE per crow. The price doubles with each pitcher: 0.003, 0.006, 0.012, 0.024, 0.048, 0.096, 0.192, and 0.384 HYPE for pitchers 9 and 10. You also pay a small HYPE gas fee on Elysium.

What is Water?

Water is 65% of all mint HYPE. It is shared by staked crows: 7 day locks earn 1x, 14 days earn 2x, and 30 days earn 4x.

Which network is it on?

Elysium, the Hyperliquid L2 by Kinetiq (chain ID 99901). Gas and mint are paid in HYPE. Bridge HYPE from HyperEVM 1:1.

When does minting start?

The date will be announced on our X account. Turn on notifications so you do not miss it.
