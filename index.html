FAQ

Questions

What is Pebble Crows?

A collection of 4,000 fully on-chain pixel crows on Robinhood Chain. Crows are minted with proof of work: your device searches for a valid hash, and each find lets you mint one crow.

Do I need a gaming PC?

No. Any modern browser can search. A graphics card makes the search faster, but phones and laptops can find pebbles too, especially when the target eases.

What does the whitelist give me?

A whitelisted wallet finds its first pebble instantly. After that first mint, it searches like everyone else.

Does joining the whitelist cost anything?

No. You complete a few steps on X and submit your wallet address. We never ask for a signature, a transaction or your seed phrase. Anyone who asks for those is not us.

How much does a crow cost?

The first crow costs 0.0005 ETH and the price rises slightly with every mint, up to 0.02 ETH for crow #4,000. You also pay normal network gas.

What is Water?

Water is 65% of all mint ETH. It is shared by staked crows: 7 day locks earn 1x, 14 days earn 2x, and 30 days earn 4x.

When does minting start?

The date will be announced on our X account. Turn on notifications so you do not miss it.
